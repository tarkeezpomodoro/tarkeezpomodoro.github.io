Privacy Policy

Updated: November 25, 2026

We respect your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you use Tarkeez Pomodoro.

Data that is stored

Your tasks, settings, stats and chosen language are saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage), on your device only. They are not sent to any server of ours, and we cannot see them. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or collect names or email addresses.
  • We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising.
  • We currently use no analytics or advertising tools.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations, under its own privacy policy.
  • Fonts: we use Google Fonts, so your browser requests the font files from Google's servers.

Notifications

If you turn on browser notifications in Settings, they are shown by your browser to tell you a round has ended. We send no notifications from a server.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will update this page beforehand and explain what changes.

Contact

For any privacy question, open a request on our GitHub feedback page.
